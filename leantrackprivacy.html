LeanTrack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

LeanTrack calculates body-fat estimates on your device. Your profile and measurements stay in the app's local storage. Ad-supported versions also use Google AdMob to display banner ads; that service processes the advertising data described below.

Your body measurements are not sent to Google AdMob. We do not use your gender, age, height, weight, body-fat results, goals, or measurement history to target ads. Older ad-free versions do not use the advertising features described in this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

LeanTrack stores your profile (gender, age, height, activity level and units), entered weight/tape/caliper measurements, calculated results, history, goals and settings locally. There is no LeanTrack account or developer-operated cloud sync. Calculations work offline. Android device backup or restore may copy app data according to your device and Google account settings.

2. Advertising and third-party data

Ad-supported versions use the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform. To serve, measure and protect ads, Google may collect and share your IP address (including approximate location inferred from it), device and advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions, and diagnostic/performance information. Advertising services require internet access. This is separate from the body measurements stored by LeanTrack.

Google uses this data for advertising, measurement/analytics and fraud prevention. The information processed and ads available depend on your consent, region, device settings and Google's services. Google determines retention of data it processes under its policies; deleting LeanTrack's local records does not delete data already processed by Google.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. SDK data is encrypted in transit. LeanTrack does not add a separate analytics or crash-reporting integration.

3. Advertising choices

Where required, a Google consent message is shown before ads can be requested. You can revisit available choices through Settings > Ad privacy options when that option is required for your region. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID through Android settings. Declining personalized ads does not block the calculator or your saved history; limited or non-personalized ads may still be available where permitted.

LeanTrack remains free, with no account registration, in-app purchases or subscriptions.

4. Children

LeanTrack is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an advertising service associated with LeanTrack, please contact us and consult Google's privacy controls.

5. Your control over your data

You can delete individual measurement entries or all saved measurements through Settings. To remove the complete local app profile and preferences, clear LeanTrack's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. You may also need to manage device backups separately. For information processed by Google, use Google's privacy controls and the contact options in its policy.

6. Not medical advice

LeanTrack provides estimates calculated from published formulas (U.S. Navy, Deurenberg BMI, Jackson-Pollock, Mifflin-St Jeor, Katch-McArdle). Results are estimates, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified health professional for medical guidance.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above.

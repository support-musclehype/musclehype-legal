LeanTrack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

LeanTrack is a body fat calculator that runs entirely on your device. This policy explains how your information is handled. In short: we don't collect it, because the app has no way to send it anywhere.

LeanTrack does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app requests no Android permissions at all — it has no internet access, so nothing you enter can leave your phone.

1. Information stored on your device

Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Your profile (gender, age, height, activity level, units)
  • Measurements you enter (weight, tape or caliper measurements)
  • Saved results and your body-fat history
  • Your goal and app settings (language, units)

This data is never uploaded. We cannot see it, and neither can anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or using the delete option in Settings, permanently removes it.

2. No accounts, no ads, no analytics

  • There is no sign-up and no user account.
  • The app contains no advertising and no advertising identifier.
  • The app contains no analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.
  • There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions — LeanTrack is free.

3. No internet access

LeanTrack declares no permissions in its Android manifest, including no INTERNET permission. All calculations run offline on your device.

4. Children

LeanTrack is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children. Because no data is collected, no information from any user — including children — is gathered or stored by us.

5. Your control over your data

Since your data lives only on your device, you are always in full control. You can delete individual entries in the app, clear all data from Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

6. Not medical advice

LeanTrack provides estimates calculated from published formulas (U.S. Navy, Deurenberg BMI, Jackson-Pollock, Mifflin-St Jeor, Katch-McArdle). Results are estimates, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified health professional for medical guidance.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above.

8. Contact

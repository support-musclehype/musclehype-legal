Common questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel Muscle Hype Pro?
Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the current period ends.

What happens to my routines if I stop paying?
Nothing is deleted. Routines you already created stay usable. Pro only removes the limit on creating new ones.

Where is my data stored? Can I move it to another phone?
Everything is stored locally on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync, so data does not transfer automatically between devices.

How do I delete all my data?
Settings → Clear Data permanently removes everything. Uninstalling the app has the same effect.

Is the body-fat number medically accurate?
No. It is an estimate based on the Navy circumference method and is intended for tracking trends over time, not for medical or diagnostic use.

Muscle Hype — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 July 2026

Muscle Hype (“the app”, “we”) is a workout tracking application. We built it to keep your training data on your own device. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. By using Muscle Hype you agree to this policy.

1. Data stored on your device

Everything you log stays locally on your phone in the app’s private database. This includes:

  • Workouts, exercises, sets, reps and weights
  • Body weight and body-fat entries
  • Custom routines and custom exercises
  • Your app settings (theme, language, units)

We do not upload this data to any server, and we cannot see it. There is no account and no sign-up. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Clear Data, permanently deletes this information.

2. Subscriptions

Muscle Hype offers an optional Pro subscription. Payments are processed by Google Play Billing, and subscription status is managed through RevenueCat, our billing provider. To validate and restore purchases, RevenueCat processes a randomly generated app user identifier and purchase information (such as product ID and purchase tokens). It does not receive your name, email or workout data. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device (for example, free-trial reminders). These are generated on-device and are not sent from or through our servers. You can disable them anytime in your system settings.

4. No advertising, no analytics

This version of Muscle Hype does not show ads, does not use an advertising identifier, and does not include third-party analytics or tracking. We do not build a profile of you.

5. Internet access

The app uses the internet only to communicate with the billing services described above. Your workout and body data are never transmitted.

6. Children

Muscle Hype is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Your rights

Because your data lives only on your device, you are always in full control of it. You can view, edit or delete any entry inside the app, and remove everything at once via Settings → Clear Data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new “last updated” date.

Muscle Hype — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Muscle Hype (“the app”, “we”) is a workout, weight and body-fat tracking application. We built it to keep your training data on your own device. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. By using Muscle Hype you agree to this policy.

1. Data stored on your device

Everything you log stays locally on your phone in the app’s private database. This includes:

  • Workouts, exercises, sets, reps and weights
  • Body weight and body-fat entries
  • Custom routines and custom exercises
  • Your app settings (theme, language, units)

We do not upload this data to any server, and we cannot see it. There is no account and no sign-up. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Clear Data, permanently deletes this information.

Your health and body measurements are never sent to advertising or analytics services.

2. Subscriptions

Muscle Hype offers an optional Pro subscription. Payments are processed by Apple on iOS and by Google Play Billing on Android. Subscription status is managed through RevenueCat, our billing provider.

To validate and restore purchases, RevenueCat processes a randomly generated app user identifier and purchase information (such as product ID and purchase tokens). It does not receive your name, email or workout data.

See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising

Muscle Hype shows ads to users on the free tier through Google AdMob. Ads appear in three places only: a banner below the main dashboard content, an occasional full-screen ad after you finish a workout, and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch to unlock an extra routine slot.

We deliberately never show ads during onboarding, an active workout, set entry, the rest timer, weight or body-fat entry, or any purchase or restore screen.

To deliver and measure ads, Google may process device and advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data. We do not receive this data and we do not build a profile of you.

Muscle Hype does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Ads are served without cross-app tracking.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform is shown before any ad is requested, and your choice is respected.

Details: How Google uses information from apps that use our services.

Pro subscribers see no ads at all.

4. Analytics

Muscle Hype does not use third-party product analytics or tracking SDKs. Aside from the advertising and billing services described above, no usage data leaves your device.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device. These are generated on-device and are not sent from or through our servers. You can disable them anytime in your system settings.

6. Internet access

The app uses the internet only to communicate with the billing and advertising services described above, and to load exercise demonstration media. Your workout and body data are never transmitted.

7. Children

Muscle Hype is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Ads requested by the app are marked as not directed to children.

8. Your rights

Because your data lives only on your device, you are always in full control of it. You can view, edit or delete any entry inside the app, and remove everything at once via Settings → Clear Data.

If you are in a region with data protection rights (for example the EEA, the UK, or California) and want to exercise them in relation to the services above, email us and we will help you direct the request to the relevant provider.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new “last updated” date.
